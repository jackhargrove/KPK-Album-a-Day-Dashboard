# Hargrove's review

I liked *Songs About Fucking* quite a bit, and this is more of the same. Perhaps even better. The two albums are remarkably similar. In sound and style, of course, but also in length, and also in the fact that side 1 is much better than side 2. Both have the fantastically jagged guitars, the pounding drum machine, and unique lyrical sensibility. I think my ranking here is like *Songs About Fucking* side 1 > *Atomizer* side 1 > *Atomizer* side 2 > *Songs About Fucking* side 2. That makes it difficult to decide which of the two albums is better. A compilation that took the best half hour of music from each album would be pretty killer. This is a pretty short review intro for me, but I really don’t have much to say about the album as a whole here that I have not already. It’s quite good.

“Jordan, Minnesota” has an insane sixteenth note rhythm of 123 123 123 123 12 12. It’s a cool rhythm and I like it well enough just for that. Though the “suck me” section at the end is a little hard to listen to, the rhythm and the insane guitar tone make the track. It’s a good opener. “Passing Complexion” is the poppiest track and a total banger. The high-pitched guitar tone is simply insane. The whole song sounds like it’s being made by a haunted piece of machinery. “Big Money” keeps things moving with crazy fast and skittery cymbal programming. When it’s working, it’s working. “Kerosene” is fantastic. The lyrics are depressing and very well-executed description of how boring small town life is. The high-pitched guitars are back in the best way, and they heighten so much at the beginning of each chorus. And those choruses are insane. So much stored tension. The lead guitar is so jagged in every direction I can only describe it as a ninja star. “Bad Houses” is the first miss, the lyrics are good but the mix is awful and it sounds so meager coming after “Kerosene”.

“Fists of Love” is decent, about a replacement level Big Black song. The lyrics are very simple and yet they still convey their meaning. The drums are heavy and the guitar is good, the sound is slow but imposing. “Stinking Drunk” is similar but just that much better. The drum programming stands out, and I love that it basically gets its own solo section. I find the a capella intro to “Bazooka Joe” grating. The rest of the song continues with me just a little annoyed. The music is fine, but the vocals make the song a chore for me. “Strange Things” is worse because there’s very little that even sticks in your head. The repeating shout is frustrating, and the riff just isn’t that good. Definitely the worst track. “Cables (Live)” ends things on a high note. It’s a very energetic performance to close the album.

More or less the same as *Songs About Fucking*. This doesn’t bother me because both albums are so short. But it didn’t leave me with a ton new to say.

Favorite tracks: Jordan, Minnesota, Passing Complexion, Big Money, Kerosene, Stinking Drunk, Cables (Live)

Least favorite track: Strange Things
