# Hargrove's review

This album is aight. Hardcore’s still not really my thing, but I’m learning. “Rise Above” is an all-time classic of any genre that’s going on all of my playlists. “TV Party” is really fun, and is shockingly catchy for a hardcore record. “Gimmie Gimmie Gimmie” I think works really well, Rollins’ inflection on the chorus sounds genuinely deranged, and the stripped-back drum bridge is awesome. I liked the lyrics on “Depression” and the vocal delivery on the chorus. The slow, sludgy guitar sections of “Life of Pain” are fantastic and foreshadow *My War*. The instrumentals generally on this album play a much stronger role than they did on that Germs album, and there are a few songs where the bass is really overpowering in a good way. The guitar is completely atonal in some of the riffs and just never finds a home, which keeps you on edge. The drums are rattling and rolling at frantic pace. Rollins’ vocals are equal parts annoying and invigorating. The album does get pretty tedious by the end, even despite its brevity. I really don’t think hardcore, or at least not this style of hardcore, is well-suited to the album format. To be honest, I liked this just about as much as that Germs album, but I’m gonna rank it slightly above because “Rise Above” is better than any individual track on *(GI)*. It’ll be in my extracurricular roundup for this week, but I’ve listened to *My War*, Black Flag’s next record, a few times this week and I definitely liked it better. This is a quintessential hardcore album though, and a good place to start.

Favorite tracks: Rise Above, TV Party, Gimmie Gimmie Gimmie, Depression, Life of Pain, Padded Cell

Least favorite track: Thirsty and Miserable
