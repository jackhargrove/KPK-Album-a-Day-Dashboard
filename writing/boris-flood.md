# Hargrove's review

Very cool album that really doesn’t sound like anything else. I largely concur with Aidan’s assessment. I have unfortunately had my fill of one album = one song records (this our third in two weeks!), but I tried not to let that fatigue play into my feelings here. By far my favorite part of the album is the third section, where everything explodes and we’re finally exposed to some raw power. The second section is also quite good, with that that slick, syrupy guitar solo that I’m listening to right now. I think the first section is interesting. I love Steve Reich, I adore the feeling of two tapes falling out of sync, so it works for me there. At the same time, there’s a little bit of “been there, done that” for me. At the same time again, I think the execution was very well-done, and it sounds cool At the same time again again, I think I found it less rewarding with each successive listen. So let’s say I’m a little torn. I think the final section is a cool outro that lets you ponder what you just heard, but it does go on a bit long and, similar to the first section, becomes somewhat of an impediment on repeated listens. The first section at least also serves a much-needed tension building purpose. This is a cool work of art that I will certainly revisit. I think it’s awesome how varied metal can be, I never know what to expect from one album to the next. I would say that I overall feel similarly about this album as I do the Tim Hecker album, although I enjoyed the Hecker album more.

Favorite tracks: Flood (but especially sections 2 and 3)

Least favorite track: Flood (but especially section 4)
