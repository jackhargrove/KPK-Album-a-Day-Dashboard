# Hargrove's review

Man what the fuck is this album. Why does 4chan have to mix these retarded hyper-edgy records into their otherwise very good list. The lyrics on this thing are just ridiculous. They’re too over-the-top to the point of just being silly. The vocalist is trying way too hard to sound awful. I can just feel how badly these guys want to offend me. There are moments where the lyrics are genuinely kind of funny. “Die You Fuck” is my personal favorite. It’s a saga of our lovely protagonist trying every which way possible to kill some guy, but he just won’t die! I kind of enjoy the lyrics on this one, it’s a little bit funny. The others have their moments as well, but for the most part it’s just like, okay, you’ve said some fucked up shit in an obnoxious voice that I know you don’t mean. Now what? In terms of vocal performance, my favorite is on “Anal Desire”. He sells the “bleed for me, shit on me” pretty well actually. It sounds like he really wants it. I am always one to give a fair shake though, so I’ll move past the lyrics and onto the music. Pretty much every song is one simple, heavily distorted riff repeated again and again, with drums of varying quality and horns on a few songs. It’s not terrible. The style works on the first two tracks for me, if I’m being honest. It’s jagged and sharp, though not so jagged and sharp as *Songs About Fucking* (I’m still in awe of how Albini got that guitar sound, it was insane). The drums on “Die You Fuck” I would also classify as good. The repetition gets old really quickly though. This album is a one-trick pony. The only thing that adds any spice to the sound as things progress are the horns, and I would consider those a highlight. If I were feeling more charitable, I might call some sections hypnotic. The only one I’m feeling charitable enough to do that for is the title track, “Obey”. The descending riff combined with the droning static makes this sound positively apocalyptic. Unfortunately, the lyrics on this track are the edgiest on the album. I can’t take them seriously at all. It creates a clash with the genuinely dark, disturbing vibe in the instrumental. It sounds like a joke, and that’s a shame. If these guys put more effort into being genuinely creepy, disturbing, or unsettling, it could have created a cool effect with the repetitive and raw instrumentals. Instead, they just chose topics that were as edgy as they could think of, and everything kind of falls flat. It’s more listenable than some of our other albums and has some redeeming qualities (what I’ve listed, also the bait and switch at the beginning is funny). But I can’t award it very high marks overall.

Favorite tracks: Kill Them All, Die You Fuck

Least favorite track: Fuckmeat
