# Hargrove's review

I thought this album was for the most part good, except for “Long Season” which was fantastic. I love live performance, but I think there were a lot of things that don’t translate from live to recorded well. For instance, the opener “Oh Slime”, where the band slowly introduces everyone on stage, didn’t translate super well for me. Additionally, I think the music gets noticeably better as the album progresses. This makes sense for a performance, where you save your best stuff for last, but sitting at home it just makes me really hope to get to the good parts. Musically, I really liked the dreamier and more psychedelic aspects, particularly the vocals (RIP) and the violin (RIP). There were also times where the drums built really well. I’ll get into the specific songs I liked later. I don’t care as much for the more reggae aspects of this album, as it sometimes feels like they just put bass line on the ands over the rest of the music.

I think from “In the Flight” onward, this album is consistently quite good. The violins on “In the Flight” remind me kind of like when the Beatles would get more orchestra (e.g. “Eleanor Rigby”), and I thought the harder rock bit at the end was great. “Walking in the Rhythm” has one of my favorite bass lines, he just slinks up and down the staff with the occasional eighth notes, sounds really grimy. The guitar solo is also great, the repetitions of “walking the rhythm” at the are hypnotic, and the breakdown at the end is the first truly transcendent moment of the album. “Smilin’Days, Summer Holiday” has my favorite hook,  and the song is so upbeat, the saxophone (or whatever instrument it is) solo goes crazy. I love the melody in “Melody”, and as a whole, it’s probably my favorite song on disc 1. They really get into a groove and every instrument is used to its potential.

“Yurameki in the Air” is where things really pick up on this record. The vocals in this track are otherworldly, I just don’t even know how he does it. The drums get really weird a few minutes into the track, but I like it. The bass keeps things grooving and moving throughout the entire 15 minutes, even as the rest of the song changes around it. It overall feels like a jam band piece, like a Japanese Grateful Dead (Glatefur Dead), and it gets really hypnotic in the middle.

And, of course, there’s “Long Season”. Later on the list we listen to the studio version, which I have never heard and am excited for, so I won’t go too in-depth here. The keyboard line sounds like something straight out of *The Legend of Zelda* and keeps the entire composition grounded. The melody that starts about 3 minutes in is so nice. I don’t usually focus in on melodies because at a certain point it feels like they’ve all been done before, so when I hear one that sounds new it’s super cool. The drums steal the show in the first breakdown, and the violin in the second. What an achievement to do this live. I think the stunned silence of the audience at the end says it all.

Overall, I enjoyed this album, but certainly less so than our last 3 albums. “Long Season” is phenomenal, but there’s just a little too much bloat for my liking. I would place this about on par with *Wish You Were Here* for me. If this album were just disc 2, I would maybe bump it up to an 8/8.5.

Favorite Tracks: IN THE FLIGHT, Smilin’Days Summer Holiday, MELODY, Yurameki IN THE AIR, Long Season

Least favorite track: Tayorinai Tenshi

7.5/10
