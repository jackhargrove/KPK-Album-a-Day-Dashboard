# Hargrove's review

Another day, another extremely long Japanese album of guitar noodling. I just do not get what it is that /mu/ sees in these. At the very least, this was one of the better albums that got put on the list purely for being Japanese. I particularly like the first couple of tracks, which provide long deconstructions of the blues that are still enjoyable to listen to. Unfortunately, this record gets very samey, and it drags on very long. There are also parts I notice where I feel like they would be very emotionally powerful were I invested in the feelings of the album, but I don’t think Fushitsusha ever does anything to give me that kind of investment, and so those moments just pass me by. The vocals range from soft to irritatingly aggressive, while the guitar keeps its distortion at an overdrive level. A real bright spot in the band for me was the bass, which on its own made “迷子 [Lost Child]” rather interesting. It’s also kind of cool how often the bass, drums, and guitar are all out of sync, creating a kind of minimalist, Reich-like phasing effect. My feelings on this record aren’t entirely negative, but I did not come away from it feeling like my 97 minutes (per listen) were well spent. Maybe this is another one that suffers from being a live album, as I would not be so down on this if I were standing in a crowd listening. As it is, though, I don’t really see myself ever coming back to it. It’s not my cup of tea.

The album starts decently strong with “あっち [Over There]”. It has a strong, bluesy vibe that I don’t mind listening to at all. Haino plays some really nice solos, and it all builds into something cool. I don’t just like this track relative to the others, I genuinely enjoy it. “暗号 [Cryptography]” starts off slow, creating an early Sonic Youth feeling in the intro. Then, at 2:30, Haino intros a sinister, evil guitar sound. This track is decent as well, even getting quite heavy by the end. Compared to the previous two tracks, “好きにすればいい [Do As You Like]” remains soft and quiet. There’s a very expressive guitar solo in the second half that’s clearly supposed to be a main event, but for some reason it just reminds me of “Iris” by the Goo Goo Dolls every time I listen to it. It might tug at my heartstrings if the rest of the album had hooked me, but as it is I just can’t get much feeling out of it. “届かない [It Won’t Reach]” starts as a slow rumble that picks up into a yell after about 4 minutes. I’m not crazy about the way it gets loud though, at least compared to the first two tracks. “ふわふわ [Fluffy Fluffy]” is the track most similar to an actual song. It has a normal drum rhythm, a dreamy guitar part, and melodic, structured vocals. It’s fine, but I would just never go out of my way to listen to it. I despise “なったんじゃない [It’s Not What Happened]”. I’m obviously fine with abrasive music, but when music I already don’t love gets abrasive, it drives me insane. What a super annoying track. I’m a big fan of the central bass riff in “迷子 [Lost Child]”. It gives the first few minutes this ominous, eerie feeling. I also don’t hate when the guitar comes in and starts clashing with the bass. It’s the best example of this album’s idea done well. “ここ [Here]” starts with cool rhythmic fuckery as the guitar and bass phase in and out with each other in the first seven minutes. After that, there are moments that I enjoy, but I don’t get much out of it for it taking up 25 minutes.

Every time I listen to this record, I’m always surprised by how much I like it in the first 20 minutes, and then I spend the next 77 minutes praying for it to be over. It’s not a drop in quality that causes it, just a complete lack of excitement or motion. It’s what it is.

Favorite tracks: あっち [Over There], 暗号 [Cryptography], 迷子 [Lost Child]

Least favorite track: なったんじゃない [It’s Not What Happened]
