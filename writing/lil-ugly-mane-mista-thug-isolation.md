# Write-up

Lil Ugly Mane is the stage name of American white rapper Travis Miller, originally from Richmond Virginia. Miller began making music in his early 20s in the mid 2000s as a member of various local punk bands in the Richmond area. In 2010, he decided to start making Southern hip hop music inspired by the Memphis rap of the 90s under the name Lil Ugly Mane. He became a frequent collaborator of the hip hop collective Raider Klan, which was led by SpaceGhostPurrp and whose most notable future member was Denzel Curry. Lil Ugly Mane’s debut album, *Mista Thug Isolation*, was released exclusively on Bandcamp in 2012. It spread through word of mouth, being championed by Tyler, the Creator and Earl Sweatshirt of the LA hip hop collective Odd Future, and is now considered a landmark underground hip hop record of the 2010s. Lil Ugly Mane released various EPs and singles from 2012 to 2015, with the most notable being the multi-EP *Three Sided Tape* series; the final volume, *Third Side of the Tape*, is on the Modern section of this list. He struggled with some major mental health issues throughout that time, and his second album, 2015’s *Oblivion Access*, was quite dark. He continued to make experimental music, including a random black metal project, and was briefly admitted to a mental hospital. In 2021, he released his third album under the Lil Ugly Mane name, *Volcanic Bird Enemy and the Voiced Concern*. The album was a sudden shift away from hip hop and into 90s-inspired indie rock and psychedelia. It was very well-received.

*Mista Thug Isolation* is Lil Ugly Mane’s best-regarded project and was instrumental in reviving Memphis rap in the underground. As the harsh, experimental industrial hip hop of the underground started gaining popularity in the early 2010s, *Mista Thug Isolation*’s smooth sound and violent lyrics resonated with a new generation of underground, internet-obsessed rappers. Memphis rap was originally a style of Southern hip hop that developed in the 90s and featured slow beats, aggressive rapping, and lo-fi production. The quintessential Memphis rap project was Three-6 Mafia’s 1995 album *Mystic Stylez*, which is on this list. Memphis rap never caught on in the mainstream, being overshadowed by the popular styles on the East and West coasts, as well as the Atanta-based style of Outkast. *Mista Thug Isolation* kicked off an underground resurgence of the style in the 2010s, alongside SpaceGhostPurrp. Denzel Curry features on the track “Twistin”, and he would go on to be the most successful artist from this scene; the 2018 album *TA13OO* was Curry’s breakout success, and he’s released some of the most well-liked hip hop projects of the 2020s. Underground Memphis rap would eventually develop into genres like phonk and cloud rap, which ultimately reached the mainstream by the late 2010s and moved hip hop back to smoother sounds. Lil Ugly Mane designed the album’s cover himself.

# Hargrove's review

I really enjoyed this album. Oh man was it a fun listen. This shit is sooooooooo daaaaaaaaaamn smooooooooooth. And yet it isn’t. The production is downtempo, samples jazzy and classical instruments, and yet there’s this undercurrent of minimalist horrorcore. Lil Ugly Mane’s (hereafter referred to as “Ugly”) flow is laid-back, lethargic, sticky as hell, and yet everything he says is depressing. This album does such a good job at creating an atmosphere of over-the-top sin and indulgence, and all the loneliness and paranoia that it brings. And man is it full of bangers. A few songs that definitely would have made it onto my basement playlist (albeit maybe controversially). I’m very picky about my white rappers (not in like a woke “they’re culture vultures!!!!1111!!!!1!!” way, just because so many white rappers just try to be Eminem and I don’t love Eminem in the first place), and Ugly has the main quality that most white rappers I like have: a strong, confident, cool-as-hell flow. I first realized this when I got really into Brockhampton and loved Matt Champion; he was admittedly a below-average lyricist, but everything he said sounded so cool that I didn’t even care. He was rhyming “Tejas” with “racists” and I did not care. There are shades of this here, although Ugly is a much better lyricist than Matt Champion. He does this thing I usually don’t like where he sometimes rearranges words awkwardly to fit the rhyme scheme, but his flow is so smooth that I just don’t care when he says “‘Cause I’m more connected than the alphabet in cursive is” on “Serious Shit” or “She on my dick like flies in a box of fruit” on “Slick Rick”; in fact, it makes them some of my favorite bars! The biggest issue with this album is that it goes on too long, and the second half certainly declines in quality compared to the first, but overall I really liked this album.

The intro “Mista Thug Isolation (12th Movement)” is a cool tone-setting horrorcore atmosphere, but not as smooth as everything else, and I don’t love it. However, “Serious Shit” starts the real songs off with a bang. What a great song. I love the jazz intro and the beat is just unimpeachable, especially that horn. You cats is yellow with it, stone-ground mustard/Ugly Mane is stone-ground up and reconstructed” is a great line. Incredible song. “Maniac Drug Dealer III” has a weak hook and subpar lyrics, but I like the high pitches in the beat and the flow is stellar. “Radiation (Lung Pollution) [feat. Supasortahuman]” has a great hook by contrast, with a sleazy-as-hell saxophone. “Slick Rick” is another banger with one of the best hooks on the album melodically, and the verse is full of hilarious lyrical gem after hilarious lyrical gem. “Wishmaster” has an annoying vocal sample, but the horrorcore lyricism is scary and the beat works well. “Alone and Suffering (Interlude)” is a decent interlude, I particularly like the bounciness of the samples and its addition to the bleak atmosphere. “Bitch I’m Lugubrious” is the best song on the album; the piano sample is simply orgasmic. Bitch, I'm lugubrious, my trap game the stupidest/You acting like I'm new at this/I been sick since the uterus/I equate your uselessness to bitches that are bootyless/Your Youtube page is viewerless, it's humorous/Flyer than a stewardess, you pussies smell like tuna fish/You wanna kill me? Try and prove it but I bet you can't maneuver it” is the best string of bars that I’ve heard in a long time. The beat is perfectly minimalist, the hook is stellar, what a fantastic piece of music man.

The album takes a dip in quality after “Bitch I’m Lugubrious”, but there are great songs to come. “Cup Fulla Beetlejuice” has good hardcore vibes, but it’s nothing too impressive. “Breeze Em Out” is excellent, the instrumental is bittersweet and upbeat, and the lyrics provide an unexpected and fleeting moment of hope. “Hoeish Ass Bitch” is my least favorite track, the hook is grating and repeated too many times, and the second verse is devoid of the flow I love elsewhere. “Mona Lisa Overdrive” is a good sex song and brings back the outrageous bars from earlier; I particularly like “I'mma get the gut, get my nut/All up on your tits and butt”. “Twistin” has a great verse from Denzel Curry and a great verse from Ugly. The hook is fantastic and it sounds like Danny Brown. Very good deep cut. I like the guitar sample on “No Slack in My Mack” but it’s way too loud in the mix. However, the second verse might be the best on the album. “Lungs full of smoke and a bag full of guts/Not the innards of a Dutch just a bag full of guts (Okay)” is hilarious, and “You're the liar when you're lying on your back/With a bullet in your head thinking back on all that whack shit you said/You can never retract cause you're dead/I’m ripping you to shreds/Ugly Mane is not right in the head” is fucking INSANE. “Lookin 4 Tha Suckin” has a great harp (?) sample but the creaking bed is overbearing. Regardless, “Bitch you ain't my lady you ain't nothing we just fucking/You was just the ho I picked when I was looking for tha sucking” is a bar. “Lean Got Me Fucked Up” has a good horrorcore instrumental with a creepy as hell vocal sample. Ugly sounds genuinely lethargic and fucked up on the second verse. “Throw Dem Gunz” has a superb violin sample that makes the song. The verse is genuinely depressing, making it one of the most effective at communicating the album’s bleak lyrical themes. The closer, “Last Breath (Outroduction)”, is the best of the three short instrumentals. It has an almost hypnagogic/vaporwave quality to it, it’s super groovy, I honestly really like it.

I appreciate how this album feels so fresh and unique without sounding anything like what I think of when I think of experimental hip hop. I liked this album enough to write a full multi-paragraph review that touches on every single song, which I think says it all.

Favorite tracks: Serious Shit, Slick Rick, Wishmaster, Bitch I’m Lugubrious, Breeze Em Out”, Twistin, Throw Dem Gunz, Last Breath (Outroduction)

Least favorite track: Hoeish Ass Bitch
