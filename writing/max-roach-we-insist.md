# Hargrove's review

I did not like this album. Easily my least favorite jazz album so far. The vocals are just not very good at all in my opinion. Beyond that, the instrumental rarely rises above only okay. I think the closing tack, “Tears for Johannesburg”, is quite good. It’s a decent jazz track with above average percussion, particularly the djembe-sounding African drums. Coincidentally, it’s the only track that’s completely instrumental. Who’d’a thunk it? To be perfectly fair, I don’t even think it was really Abbey Lincoln’s fault. I can total hear her vocals working well in a different context. The vocals are just never complemented at all by the instrumental! The third track, “Triptych: Prayer / Protest / Peace” is one of the worst songs we’ve listened to across all 94 albums. Holy fuck is it annoying! Lincoln sounds stupid on the deep and loud first third, sounds like nails on a chalkboard during the screaming of the second third, and sounds static and lifeless during the glacial final third. All the while, the instrumental rarely progresses past some drum fills. Listening to this track is pure, unadulterated torture. While no other track matches up in how unenjoyable it is, a lot of them are just dull. The opener “Driva’man” kind of has a cool work song vibe, but so much of it is a capella. The two instrumental sections are fine but nothing special, and the vocals are bland, bland, bland. “All Africa” is similarly dull for its first half, but the second half is at least interesting as a prelude to the finale. This is enough to make it my third favorite track. “Freedom Day” is the song where the vocals work best. They still sound a little bit out of place, but the melody is pretty good, and the lyrics are an interesting exploration of the emotions of the end of slavery. This is an easy second favorite track for me after “Tears for Johannesburg’”. There is just so much to dislike in this album, and every listen felt like an eternity despite it running under 40 minutes. The one redeeming quality is that its closer is above average. Otherwise, not a fan of this record at all, and I find it unlikely I return to it unless I’m specifically trying to give it a fresh start.

Favorite tracks: Tears for Johannesburg

Least favorite track: Triptych: Prayer / Protest / Peace
