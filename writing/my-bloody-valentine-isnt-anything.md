# Hargrove's review

When we did *loveless* all the way back in February, I didn’t love it as much as I remembered. I still thought it was quite good, but it had been a little while since I had listened to it and there were some parts I found grating when listening to it over and over and over for a day. When we did *you made me realise*, I actually liked it quite a bit more than I thought I would, but I docked it (perhaps too many) points for being so short and insubstantial. Our final mbv album on the list, *Isn’t Anything*, is one I’ve been aware of since listening to *loveless* for the first time in 2017 (unlike *you made me realise*), but I had never listened to it. It just didn’t seem likely to me that it would give me something *loveless* didn’t. Listening to it now, I was right and wrong. It definitely isn’t as good as *loveless*. It’s not particularly close. However, I definitely think it’s worth listening to if only because it has a few really nice songs on it. Most of this album follows the same gimmick they established in *you made me realise*: take an indie rock song and add a guitar distorted past the point of recognition (this is technically true of *loveless* as well, but 1. the indie rock gets buried under so much noise it feels like a totally different thing and 2. the execution is clinical so the composition process is less apparent). Half of the album pulls this gimmick off perfectly, creating some really pretty, dreamy, and stirring pop bangers. The other half does not pull it off, primarily thanks to lackluster songwriting, and can be a bit of a slog. The high points make *Isn’t Anything* worth a listen, but the inconsistency makes it decidedly worse than *loveless*. I would also put *you made me realise* over it just because its brevity means there isn’t room for any subpar songs. Its title track and “Thorn” are also better than anything on *Isn’t Anything*. Overall, decent but not crazy good album. And also there’s drums, and those drums are consistently one of the best parts.

The album kicks off with one of its best songs, “Soft as Snow (But Warm Inside)”. The lyrics are a super clumsy description of sex, but otherwise it fires on all cylinders. It’s got a great vocal melody and a guitar sound that I simply cannot describe. I like it a lot. The best part of “Lose My Breath” is the “ooooooooooh, ooooooooooh” section. It doesn’t have a ton more going for it, but that part is so catchy to me that it makes it to my favorites list. “Cupid Come” is a miss. The noisy part at the end are cool, but otherwise its the first to not do anything particularly interesting within the central gimmick. “(When You Wake) You’re Still in a Dream” is quite good though. Th guitar is just so loud and oppressive and overbearing, it’s just great. The vocal melody also kicks ass. “No More Sorry” is sparse and haunting. It’s my favorite of the three that I think of as personal Bilinda-driven tracks (“Lose My Breath”, “No More Sorry”, “Several Girls Galore”). “All I Need” is kind of a slog. It’s very much lacking in the actual song department. It’s my least favorite track. “Feed Me With Your Kiss” is a total banger. I love the vocals getting traded back and forth, I love how loud and fuzzy the guitars are, and I love how upbeat and energetic it is. “Sueisfine” isfine. Not great, not terrible. Same goes for “Several Girls Galore”. “You Never Should” is the last very good song on the album. It feels like it should be forgettable, but it just does the basic formula so well. I like the ascending vocal line in particular and the guitar has just enough fuzz. “Nothing Much to Lose” is the opposite: does the gimmick poorly. It blows, unfortunately. “I Can See It (But I Can’t Feel It)” is a low-key closer and is okay.

It’s nothing crazy but I’m glad I finally listened to it. The most productive part of our day on this album was that it had me revisit *loveless* and *you made me realise* and I think both need to be bumped up higher on my list ranking. *Isn’t Anything* goes below both of them.

Favorite tracks: Soft as Snow (But Warm Inside), Lose My Breath, (When You Wake) You’re Still in a Dream, No More Sorry, Feed Me With Your Kiss, You Never Should

Least favorite track: All I Need
