# Summary

Year: 1988  
Genre: Noise pop, post-punk, shoegaze

# Write-up

Story/Fun Facts: This is our first album by a repeat band, meaning I already did an elaborate backstory on mbv, so I’ll keep this one short and sweet. After fucking around for 10 years, mbv finally got their shit together and signed to a record label (Creation Records) in 1988. Bilinda Butcher or, as Carms would say, “mbv bitch”, was also added to the band as a vocalist. Their work pre-Creation was often compared to hardcore and post-hardcore bands like Hüsker Dü, but they began pioneering the sound they would be remembered for around this time. They also took influence from the jangle pop bands that were big in the mid-80s like The Smiths, as well as the noise rock and burgeoning indie of Sonic Youth and Dinosaur Jr. The EP *you made me realise* was their first release on Creation, and contained the first concrete hints of what their sound would become (the term “shoegaze” would not be coined until 1991). The EP was a commercial success, debuting at #2 on the UK indie charts. During live performances of the songs from this EP, the band would play singular chords as loudly as possible for up to 15 minutes at a time, enraging but ultimately hypnotizing the audience and setting the stage for shoegaze.

# Hargrove's review

I’ve never actually listened to any of mbv’s work other than *loveless* (not even *Isn’t Anything*, which is the one you’re supposed to listen to), so this was pretty fun. It didn’t blow me away like *loveless* did when I was 16 but, then again, nothing can really affect me like it did when I was 16; “sound of silver talk to me, makes you wanna feel like a teenager, but then you remember the feelings of a real-life emotional teenager, then you think again”. That being said, I did really enjoy this album. Not only was it cool to see the fossil record in the evolution from post-punk and post-hardcore to shoegaze, but everything just sounds kind of nice. Was a relaxing listen for me. I struggled with the length. At first I didn’t think it would be fair to call it insignificant for being so short because Charli XCX’s *Vroom Vroom* is only 12 minutes long; but, it eventually occurred to me that though *Vroom Vroom* is badass as fuck and super influential, I admire *Pop 2* and *Charli* more because they were able to keep up that energy and quality for much longer. So, in short, I am allowing the brevity to impact my opinion. It’s very pretty music, in many ways prettier than *loveless*, but it just doesn’t get me out of my seat.

I like all 5 songs. The opener starts so aggressively, but the vocals are more reflective and pensive. I really like the vocal melody and how uneven the groove is. The breakdown in the middle is cool in an experimental way (I believe that at their concerts, this is basically what they would sustain at max volume for 15 minutes at a time), but it’s not terribly enjoyable for me. “slow” has a really interesting chord progression to me, and I do love that undulation in the background. The structure is odd but it does a great job at building tension; the verses keep coming and you keep expecting a chorus or some other kind of variation, but it never comes. I enjoy the unease. “thorn” is so sunny sounding and I really enjoy it, might be my favorite on the album. The drums are crisp, and it really reminds me of Kero Kero Bonito circa *Time ’n’ Place*. It’s followed by “cigarette in your bed”, probably my least favorite track. The wall of guitar is cool at points, but this track is too downbeat, too quiet, not interesting to me. “drive it all over me” is a great closer, I really like the vocals as well as the vocal melody. It’s very much carried by those vocals, but they’re good enough for me on their own.

Pretty neat little EP. I enjoyed it a lot more than I expected; I went into it thinking it would just be a meh little test-run by a very well-regarded band, but I found myself digging it the entire listen. I’m excited for *Isn’t Anything*, I’m hoping that it’s a full-length and put together version of this sound. If it is, I might like it more than *loveless*.

Favorite tracks: slow, thorn, drive it all over me

Least favorite track: cigarette in your bed
