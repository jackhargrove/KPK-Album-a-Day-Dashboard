# Hargrove's review

Krautrock is interesting. Obviously I got super into Can’s “Vitamin C” earlier this year. I like drums a whole lot, and it’s an entire genre known for its drum beats, so why shouldn’t I like it? This album is a classic of the genre that i had heard of, but never listened to. I think it’s only okay. The three tracks that are like actual music are quite interesting, but they have their flaws. The three tracks that are ambient experiments are really not that great. On my first two listens, I really didn’t fond much to like in the album at all. Yes, the motorik beat in “Hallogallo” and “Negativland” does drive the music forward constantly, but there’s something unsatisfying about how every other aspect of the music is aimless (with the exception of some parts of “Negativland”). It feels like a missed opportunity at times. Starting with my third listen, I started to appreciate “Hallogallo” more. There’s a lot of creativity here and the rhythmic focus is appreciated. But it’s hard to rank this album particularly high, and I don’t see myself returning to it much at all.

A big issue with “Hallogallo” is that the squishy-sounding guitar is kind of annoying. Otherwise, there are a lot of good elements. I like the space rock guitar that sounds like it could be in *2001*, and the beat is obviously very cool. It’s the most consistently driving, but more than anything else I love those cymbal crashes. As I said before, I do wish the instruments around the beat had more focus and purpose. However, I came to appreciate how they used the beat as a backbone around which to noodle. It’s a good track. “Weissensee” sounds the most like a conventional, non-krautrock song. It has the space rock guitar again, but combines it with a laid back vibe not found elsewhere. It’s decent. “Negativland” is alright but ultimately worse than “Hallogallo”. It’s one major advantage is that it has a guitar line as driving as the drums. However, a little after the first half, things take a turn for the worse. Then, near the end, it gets really fast again and it’s great. It’s just a very inconsistent track.

The ambient half of this album is subpar, and that those tracks are interspersed with the real ones makes this a difficult album to listen to. “Sonderangebot” is the worst offender. It’s a whole lot of not much. “Im Gluck” is better if only because the strings are a nicer texture. “Lieber Honig” is the best of the three. There’s something haunting about the vocals that always gripped me.

I enjoyed this and I’m glad I listened to it, but it just made me want to listen to more krautrock that was hopefully better. I need something a little more refined and more focused.

Favorite tracks: Hallogallo, Weissensee, Negativland

Least favorite tracks: Sonderangebot
