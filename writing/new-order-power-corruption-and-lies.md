# Write-up


# Hargrove's review

I did enjoy this, but I’ll be heretical and say the *Substance* singles compilation was better. It’s not really a fair comparison, but the stuff I really like from New Order is their out-and-out dance stuff. I love “Bizarre Love Triangle”, “Sub-culture”, “Blue Monday”, “The Perfect Kiss”: the kinds of records they consciously only put out as singles. I think this album works well as an album, but some of the songs just don’t stick with me in the way I want from synthpop. “Your Silent Face”, “Age of Consent”, and “Leave Me Alone (in that order form my favorite to least favorite) are all beautiful, synthy post-punk tracks in the vein of Joy Division’s “Love Will Tear Us Apart Again”. I think they’re just gorgeous. “Your Silent Face” is my favorite song on the record, it’s just a stellar slow burn with a synth line just a little prettier than the one on “Age of Consent”. I also love the jarring moment when Sumner says “piss off”. Really great track, and I will be listening to it for a long time. “Age of Consent” is also quite good despite its whack-ass name. The synth is just angelic and it’s a high watermark for how beautiful synthpop can sound. “Leave Me Alone” is my least favorite of the three songs that jump out at you on first listen, but it’s still very good. Sumner sounds aching, it’s one of my favorite vocal performances from him. I also appreciate ending the album on kind of a downer, it really drives home the “pretty but depressing” vibe that New Order does so well. The “deep cut” that most impressed me was “The Village”. I’m not generally a big fan of New Order’s lyrics, but the ones here really get me. I love the sweet imagery of love in the chorus, and the reference to Ian Curtis at the end is a gut punch. Instrumentally, it’s also a great balance between their post-punk and dance modes. Really, really good track. the quality beyond those four tracks is a little iffy unfortunately. While I respect and appreciate its experimentation, the little two minute synth intro is not my favorite thing to listen to every time I put on the track. the music that follows is the most similar to their later singles output, and it’s alright, but it lacks the punch and memorability of say “Blue Monday”. “Ultraviolence” and “Ecstasy” are both good album tracks that I enjoy while listening to but remember very little of after (except the somewhat annoying vocoder stuff on the latter). “We All Stand” is my least favorite track, it’s just too slow and static. Unfortunate choice for a second track. Overall, I found this album really quite pretty, and I enjoyed taking the day to listen to it. I intend to add “Age of Consent”, “The Village”, and “Your Silent Face” to a bunch of playlists, but I don’t know how often I’ll return to the album as a whole. Still, New Order’s vibe is so immaculate, and the beautiful synth highs aren’t replicated by really any other band. Solid album.

Favorite tracks: Age of Consent, The Village, Your Silent Face, Leave Me Alone

Least favorite track: We All Stand
