# Write-up

Panda Bear (real name Noah Lennox) is a musician from Baltimore who is best known for his work as a member of Animal Collective. He took the name Panda Bear from his habit of drawing pandas on cassette tapes as a teenager. As a child, he initially learned piano, then cello, and then became a member of his high school’s choir, the latter of which would significantly affect his approach on *Person Pitch*. He and the other guys who would go on to form Animal Collective collaborated musically throughout the 90s, with 2000’s *Spirit They’re Gone, Spirit They’re Vanished* being their first collaborative release. On that album, Panda Bear only contributed drums (“perfect percussion”), but he would become the secondary creative of the band with Avey Tare. In 2007, starting with his solo album *Person Pitch* and Animal Collective’s *Strawberry Jam*, Panda Bear started using the sampler as his primary instrument, moving toward a dubby, harmonic sound. In 2013, he featured on Daft Punk’s *Random Access Memories*, singing on “Doin’ it Right”. Although Animal Collective was based in NYC in its early days, Panda Bear moved to Lisbon, Portugal in 2004, where he met his wife and had his children. He got divorced in 2022, a subject which he examined in his 2025 album *Sinister Grift*, which has created a mild career renaissance.

In the longstanding back and forth between guitars and electronics in the collective imagination of indie kids, *Person Pitch* began a major shift back into the electronics direction that would dominate early 2010s indie. When Panda Bear moved to Lisbon in 2004, customs held onto his guitar and he was unable to take it with him. However, he was able to keep his Boss SP-303 sampler, which then became his primary instrument. He was inspired to buy the sampler in the first place because of Madlib, the alternative hip hop producer best-known for his work in Madvillain. Over the next two years, with the time he got in between Animal Collective tours, Panda Bear built *Person Pitch* slowly and almost entirely out of samples ranging from classical music to reggae to Kylie Minogue. This style of production is known as “plunderphonics”, and we’ll see it again on this list with records like The Avalanche’s *Since I Left You*. He also sampled his own vocals and overdubbed them again and again, creating sunny Beach Boys vocal harmonies over each track. The result was 45 minutes of psychedelic bliss, sounding incredibly 60s pop and incredibly 00s indie at the same time. Panda Bear included a list of over 100 influences on the album spanning a wide array of genres. In turn, *Person Pitch* would be extremely influential on both late 00s/early 10s indie as well as the burgeoning hypnagogic pop/chillwave/vaporwave scene. Grimes (who straddles both) in particular listed *Person Pitch* as one of the “5 albums that changed my life”, saying that hearing this album only once made songwriting much easier for her.

# Hargrove's review

I love this album, and I have for a long time. I honestly think this is what *Smile* wishes it could be. When I listen to this, it takes harmonic pop in a progressive direction in a way that the Beach Boys never could. Not only is this album absolutely gorgeous from front to back, but it takes you through the music at an appropriate pace. The pacing of the first half of the album is phenomenal. I think it loses steam a bit in ‘Good Girl / Carrots”, but it sticks the landing with “Ponytail”. It’s so pretty, it’s hard for me to think about anything else. “Comfy in Nautica” introduces you to the harmonies beautifully, focusing n the vocals more than anything else. This isn’t a song I would seek out on its own, but wow does it prepare you fr what’s to come like no other track could. “Take Pills” is one of my favorite songs of all time. The melodies are just otherworldly. It’s actually insane. I have had the vocal melodies from this stuck in my head all day, Just gorgeous in a way I didn’t think was possible. “Bros” is a masterpiece and the best song on this album. It does to pop what King Crimson did to rock, but in such an unpretentious way that I love it so much more. It explores ideas in pop that no album as come close to sense. The melodic motif is so catchy, and the middle section with various samples is extremely well-done. “I’m Not” is a beautiful bit of ambient pop, with overdub after overdub worming its way into my ear canals. “Good Girl / Carrots” starts off a bit rough, but it eventually becomes an epic that is not quite (but almost) on the level of “Bros”. “Search for Delicious” is where things really fall apart (relatively speaking), with the focus on sample overtaking the overall beauty. But “Ponytail” ends things wonderfully, with my jaw still agape. I *adorex* this album. I would continue writing ad nauseum were I not so drunk. But fuck, man. This is just so, so beautiful. It’s a masterful work of art. It’s so different than what Animal Collective was doing too. I love, love, love, love, love it.

Favorite tracks: Comfy in Nautica, Take Pills, Bros, I’m Not, Good Girl / Carrots, Ponytail

Least favorite track: Search for Delicious
