# Hargrove's review

Wow I did not like this album. It was really frustrating for me because usually I can handle any amount of experimentation so long as I can see where it’s headed. And I could see where it was headed super clearly, this was obviously extremely influential on Animal Collective, Joanna Newsom, and other members of the freak folk movement of the 2000s. I love Animal Collective and Joanna Newsom and some others. But this album just rubbed me the wrong way, although not even right off the bat. I kinda like the first track “Blue Mamba”, totally felt like a set up for an album I would like: frantic guitar, driving drums, folk aesthetic.

But then we got track 2, “Tarmac 23”. Fuck this shit. Positively dreadful. The voices get worse every time I listen. The guitar is meandering and uninteresting to me. It doesn’t even have the charm that Captain Beefheart had. But it’s nothing compared to “Café Batik”. HATE HATE HATE HATE HATE. Like, shut up man. What are the vocals even supposed to do for me? The chords are cool, I guess. And you wanna know another dogshit track? “Papa Legba”. This song is why honkeys shouldn’t be allowed to play the blues. “The Vinegar Stroke” is fine but it’s forgettable and insignificant. “Burial in the Sky” is atmospheric, I guess? Yeah, the atmosphere of THE MOON. Gottem!

There were a few tracks that I liked, and I’m glad I stuck through the album despite my overall disdain because there’s almost always something positive to be gleaned. “Radar 1941” is genuinely a really good song, not just relatively good for the album. It’s got that dark surf and somewhat Arabic vibe of “Misirlou”, and it’s rally nice. Cymbals are, of course great. Real bright spot for me. I also rather liked “Space Prophet Dogon”. The vocals actually sound appealing to me, and the guitar pairs really well. “Esoterics of Abyssinia” is alright, the guitar is at least interesting. I’m pretty neutral on “The Shining Path”, the whistled melody is really cool but everything else is whatever.

I really gave this album a chance. Gave it as many listens as I could today. I did find some nice points, but just not for me. “Radar 1941” and “Space Prophet Dogon” keep this above *Trout Mask Replica* for me, but only by a thread. Everything else is just as unpleasant to me, but without an aesthetic I at least appreciate. I am pretty stoked that AnCo and JoNew got something out of it though.

Favorite tracks: Space Prophet Dogon, Radar 1941

Least favorite track: Papa Legba
