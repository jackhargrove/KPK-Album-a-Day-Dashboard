# Hargrove's review

This album didn’t really move me very much, and I’m going to keep this pretty brief because, genuinely, I don’t have a ton to say.

I’ll start with the songs that stood out to me and that I enjoyed. I really liked “Chocolate Matter”, the guitar line is loud and great, and there is an overall sunny, Beach Boys vibe to the vocals. Definitely my favorite track on the album. I also really like the closing track, “Design: 2: 3”. Again, it’s a track driven by a phenomenal and rocking guitar line, at least in the first half (my favorite half). I respect the glitch that separates the first part from the second part, even if the second part is less exciting; although, the second part does have some really nice harmonies at the end. I really like the beat for “Dedicated”, that was the part that reminded me the most of Iglooghost. It was chaotic and frenetic and really groovy. And a song that I kept going back to was “Sept”, which had the most beautiful vocal melody on this record.

Other than that, though, nothing really stuck out to me, neither good nor bad. The song I chose as my least favorite just happened to be one I wasn’t enjoying as I was writing this. It just feels really one-note, and it doesn’t stick in my mind much at all once a song has passed or once the album is over (to the degree that writing this review is difficult). I don’t really detect this cool aesthetic that you guys are getting. At the same time, I don’t hate it! It mostly washes over me, and is enjoyable without being particularly interesting.

Overall, I’m just a little confused by this pick. Even when I haven’t loved an album so far, I’ve been able to understand what people see in it, why it’s up there. Here I’m just baffled. It must be because the makers of this list are evil pretentious douchebags who have ulterior motives and don’t actually appreciate art like they say they do, unlike me.

Favorite tracks: Sept, Dedicated, Chocolate Matter, Design: 2: 3

Least favorite tracks: To All the Dancers of the World, a Round Form of Fantasy
