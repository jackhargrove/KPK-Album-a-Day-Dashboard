# Write-up

Year: 1966

Genre: Chamber Pop, Psychedelic Pop

Story/Fun Facts: Beach Boys were a super popular surf group with popular songs like “Surfing USA” and “Barbara Ann”. Lead singer Brian Wilson felt burned out in 1965 and decided not to tour with the other Beach Boys. He holed up in a studio, started taking acid regularly, and became deeply mentally unwell. He also heard *Rubber Soul* by the Beatles, a shockingly complex album from a competitor band. Brian Wilson took that as a personal challenge and wrote the songs for *Pet Sounds* all by himself. When his bandmates returned from tour, they were super confused by the whole thing but Brian Wilson was the leader and they put it out anyway. Ended up becoming the inspiration for The Beatles’ *Sgt. Pepper’s Lonely Hearts Club Band*. Afterward, Brian Wilson wanted to keep going in the same direction with a new album *Smile* featuring the song “Good Vibrations”, but his mental illness and drug addiction made him go genuinely insane and got him permanently kicked out of the band (foreshadowing for *Wish You Were Here* in 2 days). Go figure.

# Hargrove's review

Song Ranking:

1. “God Only Knows” - Perfect song. Crazy how quickly the chorus resolves the palpable tension from the verses. God Only Knows what I’d be without this song.
2. “Caroline, No” - Inspires a deep sadness within me. I’ve always really loved this song but can never fully articulate why.
3. “I Just Wasn’t Made for These Times” - He really wasn’t, and the times consumed him. The mustard he puts on the “wasn’t” and the “for these” in the chorus is insane. Theremin makes me feel like I’m in Star Trek
4. “Wouldn’t It Be Nice” - It would. Sounds as beautiful as the picture it paints. Introduces the insane harmonies that’ll be omnipresent throughout. Drums coming in and out is cool too.
5. “I Know There’s An Answer” - Drug song as Brian Wilson was frying his brain on drugs. Mike Love is a sissy. The saxophone part is disgusting. The breaks where it’s just a bassline sound like a seedy spy movie. Cool and complex track.
6. “Don’t Talk (Put your Head on My Shoulder)” - Brian really enunciates his words here. The strings that start in the second verse and then build for a while are haunting.
7. “You Still Believe in Me” - A little slow for my tastes but great melody. Chorus feels so unresolved (in a good way). Harpsichord? Outro goes a little long but apparently Paul McCartney liked that.
8. “Let’s Go Away for a While” - Timpani and vibraphone carry
9. “Here Today” - Percussion and backing vocals are a bit overbearing. Love the bassline though. The vocals in the pre-chorus feel frantic in a good way.
10. “Pet Sounds” - I played a guiro once in 4th grade band, will never forget that sound
11. “That’s Not Me” - The percussion is really off-kilter, I like that. I find the vocal melody kind of repetitive.
12. “I’m Waiting for the Day” - Sparse instrumentation during choruses, dense after, good dynamic. Brian can hit some high notes.
13. “Sloop John B” - I think this song sounds better at a higher tempo than they play it. Very clearly meant to be the hit, sounds more like the surf stuff they did prior than the rest of the album. Still quite good though!

Favorite tracks: Wouldn’t It Be Nice, Don’t Talk (Put Your Head on My Shoulder), God Only Knows, I Know There’s an Answer, I Just Wasn’t Made for These Times, Caroline, No

Least favorite track: Sloop John B
