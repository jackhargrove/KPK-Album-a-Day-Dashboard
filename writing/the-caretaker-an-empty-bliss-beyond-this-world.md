# Write-up


# Hargrove's review

I remember back when the Caretaker’s *Everywhere at the end of time* was a TikTok trend. I had always kind of wanted to listen to it, but six and a half hours is a lot of time to set aside. I’ve been looking forward to *An empty bliss beyond this World* as it seemed like a similar concept but in a much more reasonable time frame. While I do think it was interesting to listen to, my biggest issue is the lack of a feeling of progression. The idea of a descent into dementia fascinates me, but that feeling just wasn’t strongly evoked in me here. I get that the memory loss in dementia is cyclical and there are moments of lucidity, but I had a conceptual issue with “An empty bliss beyond this World” and “Camaraderie at arms length” (even though both are in my favorite tracks). They removed a lot of my feelings of progression. Worse, though, is that even the most fucked up tracks aren’t that far gone. There are never any that lose their musical quality, the loops just get a little shorter and a little more veiled by snaps, crackles, and pops. Now, I do think I’m being a little unfair; I’m judging this album against expectations I had formed for an entirely different album. There are some genuinely creepy moments on here, particularly “I feel as if I might be vanishing” and “Pared back to the minimal”. The Caretaker’s decision to use ballroom music like this for his project is also just genius. Even before his manipulation, my brain imbues the samples with a wistful, lost quality. It’s not just that the music is super old, but also that it was largely forgettable even at the time it was released. It hasn’t left its legacy as a standard, nor as an important influence on the music to come. It’s just been lost to time, as most people will be. Now, *that* fucks me up. Overall, this is a project with a high conceptual floor, it’s just that *An empty bliss beyond this World* often stays near that floor instead of approaching its ceiling. I hope to listen to *Everywhere at the end of time* in the not-too-distant-future, and I hope that gets closer to this concept’s potential.

“All you are going to want to do is get back there” starts things off very well. The sample was a great choice, particularly for that lonely saxophone. “Moments of sufficient lucidity” already has a sparser sample loop and more white noise. I don’t care for the sample on “The great hidden sea of the unconscious”, making it my least favorite. “Libet's delay” gets rid of some of the fuzz to bring us back to reality and has perhaps the best sample on the album. However, my favorite track has to be “I feel as if I might be vanishing”. That loop is CREEPY. I really have to commend the Caretaker on this one, I’ve been worried that some of my other favorites are largely just because of the quality of sample, but this was a brilliant way to loop this. “An empty bliss beyond this World” has the character of an old, abandoned carousel still spinning through the cobwebs. It’s the exemplary track I would show someone to introduce them to this album. “Bedded deep in longterm memory” begins to play with stereo, and also has the shortest loop so far. “A relationship with the sublime” is not all that interesting. “Mental caverns without Sunshine” is quite atmospheric and the sound starts to get really distant. “Pared back to the minimal” feels like the low-point of the album memory-wise, and I appreciate its conceptual value. Both reprises are good conceptually, but “An empty bliss beyond this World” is the only one that adds anything to its original track. I also wish they had been more spaced out. “Camaraderie at arms length” is good for the same reason the opener is good. I appreciate that the sample in “The sublime is disappointingly elusive” is actually a finale, but I do question the point of this track’s existence.

Strong concept, middling execution. It was pleasant to listen to at points though.

Favorite tracks: All you are going to want to do is get back there, Libet’s delay, I feel as if I might be vanishing, An empty bliss beyond this World (and reprise), Pared back to the minimal, Camaraderie at arms length

Least favorite track: The great hidden sea of the unconscious
